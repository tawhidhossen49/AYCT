Questions

Before you play.

Who can take part?

The 32 players invited to this year's edition. The tournament admin creates every account, so there's no public sign-up.

How do I sign in?

With the email and password your tournament admin gives you. Players, moderators, commentators and admins all use the same sign-in page.

What if I miss the start of my game?

The board unlocks at the scheduled time and white's clock starts straight away. If a player never shows up, their clock runs out and they lose on time.

What happens if a knockout game is drawn?

Knockout games need a winner, so the moderators hold a tiebreak and record who goes through. The bracket moves them on automatically.

How are ties in a group decided?

By Sonneborn-Berger score first, then the number of wins, then the games between the tied players, then the order of the draw.
