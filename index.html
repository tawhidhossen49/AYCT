The story

Questions

Before you play.

Who can take part?

Any Bangladeshi citizen who is currently a student, is under 30 and has an active Lichess account. Register through the official form by 10 January 2027; the entry fee is 50 BDT.

How does the Selection Round work?

It is played on Lichess on 4 February 2027 at 7:30 PM: a Swiss of 10 rounds at 5+0. The top 32 qualify and are ranked 1 to 32 by their finish. That rank places them in the groups and settles colours and close calls later on.

How do I sign in to the portal?

Qualifiers get an email and password from the organisers. Players, moderators, commentators and admins all use the same sign-in page.

What if I miss the start of my game?

Be online 15 minutes before the start. White must make the first move within 5 minutes of the start, and Black within 5 minutes of White's first move. A player who misses that deadline loses the game by forfeit; if neither player turns up, both forfeit. The clocks start once both players have moved.

How do the knockout rounds work?

Every tie is a two-game series with colours reversed, and the final is four games, all of them played. The better-ranked player has White in game one. If a series is level, one 3+2 tiebreak game is played with the colours of the last game reversed. If that is drawn too, a 3+2 decider: the lower-ranked player picks the colour, and a draw sends the higher-ranked player through.

How are ties in a group decided?

Points first (win 1, draw ½), then Sonneborn-Berger, then the number of wins, then the games between the tied players, then the better Selection Round rank.

What if I lose my connection?

Your clock keeps running, so rejoin from the same link as fast as you can. If the platform itself fails, the arbiters decide how the game continues.

What are the fair-play rules?

No engines, opening books or outside help during a game. Breaking the fair-play rules is penalised, up to forfeiting games or disqualification. Report a problem to the arbiter within 5 minutes of the game; appeals go to the organisers within 15 minutes of a decision.

Can I offer a draw?

Yes. Draw offers are allowed, and an offer stands until your opponent accepts it, declines it or makes a move.
